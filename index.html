Aug 2025 - Present

AI-Guided UGV Stone Fruit Harvesting System

This project aims to develop an automated fruit harvesting arm designed to address the growing labor shortages in agriculture and to reduce the significant produce waste resulting from unharvested crops. The proposed system integrates a multi-degree-of-freedom gripper arm, an extension arm, a telescopic arm mechanism, and a computer-vision subsystem capable of identifying and localizing ripe fruit in real time. Through iterative concept generation and evaluation, it was decided to use a suction-based gripper, an extension, and a telescopic arm optimized for reach and mobility.
